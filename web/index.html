One person wrote at least half of their commits in the last 12 months. What is a bus factor? The number of people who wrote at least half of an area's commits in the last 12 months, counting people with 2+ commits there and areas with 5+ such commits. A bus factor of 1 means one person wrote at least half. It measures who wrote the code, not who understands it: reviewers and people off GitHub can know it too.
